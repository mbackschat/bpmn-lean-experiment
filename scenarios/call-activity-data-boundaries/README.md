# Supplier onboarding with Call data

The [whole model](process.bpmn) passes a supplier summary into isolated due diligence, copies its declared decision back, and completes final onboarding approval while retaining a caller-only note. The [String schedule](string.scenario.json) and [explicit-null schedule](null.scenario.json) contain neutral inputs for independent Lean, TypeScript and Temporal execution under the [scalar Call profile](../../profiles/bpmn-2.0.2-call-activity-data-boundaries-draft/README.md).

Both cases retain exact source identity and mutate called-task ownership to check differential sensitivity. No CIB Call-data result or browser journey is claimed. The [capsule](../../docs/capsules/CALL-ACTIVITY-DATA-BOUNDARIES-PROPOSAL.md) owns the semantic and recovery evidence and remaining closure obligations.
