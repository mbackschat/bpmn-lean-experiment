# Interruption of an embedded region around nested Calls

This directory serves [PC-SCOPE-07](../../../docs/PRACTICAL-CIB7-COVERAGE-TARGET.md#delivery-task-list): the removal of the subtree an interrupting boundary owns (embedded scopes, Human, effect, Message and Timer waits, called instances, local and interface data), the survival of outside and sibling work and committed writes, late results addressed afterwards, and interruption around a Call inside an embedded Sub-Process. Interruption of Multi-Instance bodies is owned by PC-MI-12 and its own witnesses; PC-SCOPE-07's clause for a Multi-Instance-owned subtree is settled by [escalation-multi-instance](../escalation-multi-instance/README.md) `parallel-subprocess-interrupting`, where an interrupting boundary removes both instance bodies and keeps the outside work. The [campaign model](campaign.bpmn) is project-authored MIT source for the `cibseven-2.2.0` profile; every schedule is answer-free and CIB Seven `2.2.0` supplies the retained answer.

After a Parallel fork, the Human Tasks `IntakeNorth` and `IntakeSouth` lead to an identity-only converging Exclusive Gateway that enters the Sub-Process `Region`, so two concurrent activations of one definition can be live; the Human Task `Outside` runs beside them. `Region` declares the local `scratch` and carries an interrupting Message boundary `Abort` (message `abortRegion`) to the Human Task `Aborted`; its normal completion leads to the Human Task `AfterRegion`. Inside `Region`, three branches run: the Human Task `Draft` (whose completion may write `scratch` and the Process variable `draftNote`) followed by the Service Task `Publish`, answered by `effectResult`; a Receive Task `Quote` (message `quoteReceived`); and the nested Sub-Process `Inner`, which runs the Human Task `Review` and then the Call `Approve` of `Process_Approve` (in `scratch` as `memo`, out `verdict` as `approval`), with an interrupting `PT30S` Timer boundary on the Call to the Human Task `Escalate`. `Process_Approve` runs the Human Task `Sign`. The `Region` boundary and the nested Call boundary are addressed by their host's activation. A called instance is addressed by the kernel's derived identifier.

| Schedule | Case it exercises |
|---|---|
| [abort-one-region-mid-work](abort-one-region-mid-work.scenario.json) | Both regions open, the north region's `Draft` writes `draftNote` and `scratch` and `Review` starts its Call, then `Abort` is delivered to the north region; its `Publish`, `Sign` and `Quote` are addressed afterwards, and the south region's `Review` and its called `Sign` then complete. |
| [abort-both-regions-in-turn](abort-both-regions-in-turn.scenario.json) | `Abort` is delivered to the south region, delivered to it a second time, its `Draft` is addressed, then the north region is aborted and `Aborted` tasks and `Outside` complete. |
| [call-timeout-inside-region](call-timeout-inside-region.scenario.json) | Both regions reach their Call, the `PT30S` Timer of the north Call fires, the north called `Sign` is addressed afterwards, the south called `Sign` completes, and `Escalate` and the north `Quote` follow. |

The schedules carry no expected route, local, mapped variable, open wait or Process state; the retained CIB evidence is the answer.
