# Parallel applicants journey: applicant Sub-Process bodies side by side

Answer-free schedules for [PC-JOURNEY-04](../../../docs/PRACTICAL-CIB7-COVERAGE-TARGET.md#delivery-task-list): a case screens all its applicants at once, each body a Human Task interview followed by an automated credit check, and an underwriter decides the case once the bodies are done. A parallel Multi-Instance Sub-Process, a completion predicate that a declined credit result satisfies and an outer deadline give the empty, natural-completion, early-completion and outer-interruption cases, with equal and distinct items and interviews and credit results that finish out of order. Each schedule pairs an ordinary path with the nearest wrong-allocation, sibling-data, premature-return or revived-effect case. The [Multi-Instance](../../../docs/kernel/semantics/MULTI-INSTANCE-SEMANTICS-SPEC.md), [work](../../../docs/kernel/semantics/WORK-SEMANTICS-SPEC.md) and [data](../../../docs/kernel/semantics/DATA-SEMANTICS-SPEC.md) semantics own the meaning; the schedules only exercise it. The profile is `cibseven-2.2.0`.

The credit check is a named business handler, `checkCredit`, that the exact [source overlay](parallel-screening-overlay.json) binds to a neutral effect descriptor; the model carries no probe handler and no scenario names a model identity, so a host binds the same name through its own configuration (PC-AUTO-01). Every schedule therefore names no CIB revision (`cibRevision` is `not-applicable`): the CIB runner registers only the three delegate tokens of `CibSevenScriptedEffect.HANDLER_BEANS`, so it cannot run a model whose handler carries this name. Retained CIB evidence for this journey needs a runner that registers the token, or a calibration probe that registers it as a delegate bean; the constructs it combines have their own CIB witnesses ([multi-instance-sub-process](../multi-instance-sub-process/README.md) for the parallel bodies, their early completion and their outer Timer, [multi-instance-instance-sites](../multi-instance-instance-sites/README.md) for the item and counters inside a body).

## Model

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [parallel-screening.bpmn](parallel-screening.bpmn) | The model of the [sequential journey](../journey-sequential-applicants/README.md) with a parallel loop. The start variables are the String `caseId`, the List `applicants` of Maps (`name`, `incomeCents`), the Integers `screened`, `scoreSum` and `declines` at 0 and the Strings `lastScreened`, `lastBand`, `lastVerdict` and `lastNote` at `none`. The Sub-Process *Screen one applicant* loops in parallel over `applicants`, one body per item (the item is `applicant`), declares the body locals `verdict` and `interviewNote`, and ends its loop when `${declines >= 1}`. A body runs the Human Task *Interview the applicant* (candidate group `screeners`, publishing the applicant's name, declared income and position) and then the Service Task *Check the applicant's credit* (named handler `checkCredit`, asynchronous before), which takes the name, the income and the interview's `verdict` and `interviewNote` and returns `result`, a Map with an Integer `score` and a String `band`. Its output mappings write at the Process: `screened` plus one, `scoreSum` plus the score, `declines` plus one when the band is `decline`, and `lastScreened`, `lastBand`, `lastVerdict` and `lastNote` from the body. An interrupting Timer boundary *Screening window closes* (`P3D`) on the Sub-Process leads to the Human Task *Close the expired case* (`underwriters`). Normal completion of the Sub-Process leads to the Human Task *Decide the case* (`underwriters`). Both tasks publish the counters and the `last` values, so the surviving writes and the owner of the last write are visible at the outer return. |
| [parallel-screening-overlay.json](parallel-screening-overlay.json) | The exact source overlay binding the delegate token to the neutral operation `checkCredit`. |

The applicants the schedules use are `Mara Berger` (420,000 cents), `Tomas Okafor` (310,000), `Ines Vidal` (265,000) and `Noor Haddad` (198,000). The window is due 259,200,000 ms after the case starts at logical time 0.

## Schedules

Each schedule names the acceptance clause of PC-JOURNEY-04 it witnesses. Every command in a failing schedule that is not the refused one is the valid continuation, so a refusal that leaves the state unchanged is told from one that does not. The `last` values come from the credit result that completed last and pair that body's own name, verdict, note and band, so a body that took a sibling's interview data or a result meant for another body shows as a mismatch.

| Schedule | Clause witnessed | Case it separates |
|---|---|---|
| [empty-applicants](empty-applicants.scenario.json) | Empty case; wrong allocation fails | No applicant: the Sub-Process completes at once, so *Decide the case* is open after the start, the window was never armed and its firing is refused, and an interview and a credit result for a body that does not exist are refused. Every counter stays 0. |
| [distinct-applicants-reverse-completion](distinct-applicants-reverse-completion.scenario.json) | Natural completion; out-of-order human and effect completion; wrong allocation and sibling data fail | Three distinct applicants. The interviews finish for bodies 3, 1 and 2 and the credit results arrive for bodies 2, 3 and 1, so the first applicant finishes last: `screened` is 3, `scoreSum` sums the three results and the `last` values are the first applicant's own name, `hold` verdict, note and band. *Decide the case* opens only with the last result. |
| [equal-applicants-keep-their-own-bodies](equal-applicants-keep-their-own-bodies.scenario.json) | Natural completion; equal items; wrong allocation and sibling data fail | The first two items are equal. The second is interviewed first and its credit result arrives last, so each equal item keeps its own body, interview data and result, and the `last` values are the second equal item's own. |
| [parent-returns-only-after-the-last-body](parent-returns-only-after-the-last-body.scenario.json) | Premature parent return fails; wrong allocation fails | A credit result before any interview, an interview beyond the collection, a repeated interview, a result for a check that has not begun and a repeated result are refused; *Decide the case* is refused after the first body, after the second and while the last credit result is open, and completes the Process only after it. |
| [early-completion-on-decline](early-completion-on-decline.scenario.json) | Early completion; completed writes survive; revived cancelled effect fails | Four applicants. The first body finishes; the second interview and the fourth interview complete, so two credit checks are open beside the third interview. *Decide the case* is refused, then the fourth body's result declines: the loop ends and *Decide the case* is offered with the first and fourth bodies' writes (`screened` 2, `scoreSum` 1,190, `declines` 1). The window, the second body's open credit result, the third body's interview and a check that never began are refused. |
| [window-closes-with-bodies-in-flight](window-closes-with-bodies-in-flight.scenario.json) | Outer interruption; completed writes survive; revived cancelled effect fails | One body finished, one waits for its credit result and one for its interview. The window fired an hour early is refused, then fired at 259,200,000 it removes both open bodies and leaves *Close the expired case* with the finished body's writes. The removed credit result and interview, *Decide the case* and a second firing are refused. |
| [window-fired-after-the-loop-completed](window-fired-after-the-loop-completed.scenario.json) | Natural completion withdraws the outer window; stale firing fails | Both bodies finish in reverse interview order; a firing of the window, a repeated credit result and *Close the expired case* are refused, and *Decide the case* completes the Process. |

Notes for the target runners:

- Inner tasks and effects are addressed by activation, counted per begun occurrence of the element in the Process instance. A parallel body begins every interview at entry, in item order, so the interview of applicant k is activation k. A credit check begins when its interview completes, so its activation is the position of that completion: with interviews finishing for bodies 3, 1 and 2, the check of body 3 is activation 1. The schedule labels name both the body and the check.
- Every effect result is a scenario `effectResult` stimulus, so no handler runs: the host supplies `checkCredit` through its own configuration, and the stimulus carries each result.
- The interview submits `verdict` and `interviewNote` by name. The Sub-Process holds both as body locals, so each submission lands in its own body's scope ([CIB-EXT-0005](../../../docs/CIB-BPMN-RELATION-REGISTER.md#cib-ext-0005--public-user-task-completion-installs-submitted-process-variables)) and reaches the Process only through that body's credit check.
- The bodies write the shared Process names `screened`, `scoreSum`, `declines` and the `last` values when their credit results arrive. BPMN 2.0.2 §13.3.7 orders parallel instances by nothing, so the schedules fix each write's order by the order of their commands; each write happens at its own result command and never in the closure that begins the bodies.
- The schedules fire the window and address bodies that a refused command would leave unchanged, so the refusals are part of the schedule rather than harness mistakes.
- The schedules carry no expected route, open wait, variable or Process state.
