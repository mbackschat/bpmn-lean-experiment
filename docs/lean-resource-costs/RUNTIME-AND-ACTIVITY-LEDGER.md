## Measurement basis

These dated records retain historical measurements and diagnostic dispositions; they do not certify the current source tree. Current source-bound rows and cgroup acceptance are owned by [the executable module-cost registry](../../scripts/lean-module-cost.ts) and [the acceptance record](../../scripts/lean-memory-acceptance.ts). Apply [the current measurement procedure](../CONTRIBUTOR-SETUP-GUIDE.md#memory-bounded-lean-measurements), [execution and resource policy](../VERIFICATION-INFRASTRUCTURE-POLICY.md#lean-execution-and-resource-evidence) and [commit binding](../REVIEW-POLICY.md#commit-boundaries).

Unless a record states an exception, this series used the pinned Lean image, one CPU and Lean thread, network isolation, a fixed 3 GiB controller with no additional swap, warm dependencies and removal of only the exact target’s eight generated products, with observed target rebuilding. GNU RSS and cgroup charging are separate measures. Exit-zero results at the ceiling or with controlled events/pressure are not silently accepted; failed and skipped runs remain identified. Target-only calibration supplies no performance trend, cold full-library qualification or independent semantic approval. Historical acceptance criteria and exceptions are retained as recorded; current policy owns acceptance of new evidence.
