## Objective and boundary

Make ordinary business processes executable across the practical BPMN elements and depth implemented by CIB Seven `2.2.0`, with Lean and TypeScript agreeing on their meaning and Temporal preserving that meaning durably. [PROJECT-DESIGN](PROJECT-DESIGN.md#practical-cib7-coverage) owns the objective and [CIB7 ceiling](PROJECT-DESIGN.md#cib-seven-220-breadth-ordering). Historical MUE checkpoints supply reusable evidence; they do not establish this broader result.

The required unit is a behavioral variant in a useful combination: for example, a parallel Multi-Instance Sub-Process whose instances contain both human and automated work, rather than the presence of a Multi-Instance XML marker. Bounds and excluded combinations remain explicit. A row cannot close while its representative journey depends on a private capability, a probe-only business handler, or a hard-coded business-model identity.

## Delivery task list

This is the finite execution checklist for the target above. Each row names a required outcome with its decisive acceptance, its prerequisite contracts and its state. These are work items, not equally weighted coverage points or a replacement for the BPMN requirement, CIB profile or platform denominators.

### How a task closes

`open` means some required acceptance remains, even when a narrower slice already works. `held` means the named external or owner decision prevents closure. `done` means the row met the [definition of done](kernel/ARCHITECTURE-SPEC.md#definition-of-done-for-a-row) and names the dated review that approved it; a row closed before the kernel rewrite keeps its receipt as a commit permalink and its state only while the kernel's scenarios witness its acceptance. Design-only rows close on the reviewed bounded contract; their implementation and delivery rows remain separate.

Prerequisite columns name reviewed contracts or mechanisms, not whole-row completion edges. A row closes when its acceptance holds on accepted contracts it consumes; mutually composed rows share one reviewed contract and close jointly. A successor may develop against a reviewed prerequisite; it cannot close before the evidence it consumes is accepted. Final integrated rows consume the closed behavioral rows, avoiding a circular requirement that every behavior first pass final acceptance.

Keep IDs stable. Add discovered implementation steps under their existing outcome rather than silently expanding the target; a discovered gap in a family reopens its owning row. A genuine change to required scope, exclusions or acceptance needs a visible amendment here and the applicable independent review; never delete a difficult row, mark it optional, or narrow its acceptance to obtain completion. Report counts only from these states, never as a support percentage.

### Contract dependency key

Ranges are inclusive; slash-separated suffixes name individual rows in the same family. `DATA` means PC-DATA-01–09; `CONTROL` means PC-CF-02–08; `TASK` means PC-HUM-01–06 and PC-AUTO-01–08; `SCOPE` means PC-SCOPE-01–07 and PC-MI-01–12. `EVENTS` means the Timer, Message, Signal and Conditional rows; `EXCEPTIONS` means PC-ERROR-01–03, PC-ESCALATION-01–03 and PC-TERMINATE-01–02; `COMPENSATION` means PC-COMPENSATION-01–05. Consume only the named behavior's applicable contract, stated in the row, rather than making every member a blocking predecessor. `PUBLIC` is the published engine and platform contract, not the final qualification row.

The source basis is the [registered pristine CIB7 checkout](SOURCES.md#cib-seven) and the [practical-depth research](research/CIB-SEVEN-BPMN-BREADTH-RESEARCH.md#practical-depth-refresh). Java class and method names in the rows identify source and test precedents, not executed compatibility receipts.

### Bounded contract decisions and source restrictions

The required design tasks fix implementation choices without deleting required behavior. PC-DATA-01 must supply String/null/Boolean/integer and typed nested lists/maps, and decide a useful precise numeric amount carrier and its limits. PC-DATA-07 is bounded by the read-only JUEL subset levels of the [architecture](kernel/ARCHITECTURE-SPEC.md#runtime-state-and-step); broader context needs a reviewed amendment. PC-AUTO-05/07 use the Script and decision bindings of the [owner decisions](kernel/ARCHITECTURE-SPEC.md#owner-decisions-on-the-path); they do not authorize dependency installation or invent a language. New providers retain dependency approval. A design acceptance alone supplies no runtime coverage.

PC-SCOPE-03 must settle exact retained version binding and the disposition of latest/version-tag and tenant selectors; nested/repeated/concurrent Calls remain required independently of optional CIB API compatibility. PC-CF-08 and PC-MI-11 enumerate composition cells, not an unrestricted Cartesian product. Bounds must admit their named ordinary and separating examples and preserve forward-compatible admission. Missing source evidence or a required cell cannot disappear behind “selected”; record the reviewed source disposition and amend the target visibly if its boundary changes.

Pinned CIB allows at most one combined root None/Timer initial start and one ordinary Sub-Process Start. Message/Signal/Conditional starts have separate multiple-start accounts. Error ESP must interrupt; Escalation boundaries admit User Task, Sub-Process and Call hosts. Each Activity has at most one Compensation boundary; each Transaction has at most one Cancel boundary. Interrupting cycle Timers require a reviewed warning-safety rule under the existing warning-blocking admission policy. PC-BASIC-04 refuses unsupported Receive `instantiate=true`, multiple EventDefinitions on one Event and `parallelMultiple=true`; these differ from supported multiple separate starts/boundaries. PC-HUM-06 covers the supported addressless Receive wait.

Modeled Signal uses broadcast; external occurrence-targeted Signal is an optional API contract, not a modeled local-signal attribute. [CIB-LIM-0002](CIB-BPMN-RELATION-REGISTER.md#cib-lim-0002-modeled-bpmn-key-and-context-correlation-is-not-executed) separates practical CIB correlation criteria from the existing standards-only modeled key profile. PC-MESSAGE-08 must classify its project-owned criterion protocol; unsupported modeled compound keys cannot satisfy it. Unbound CIB Message Throw/End forms are pass-through; no Message Flow invents transport. PC-COMPENSATION-01 owns order/context/failure classification before wider Compensation; PC-TX-01 owns ordinary Transaction success without claiming protocol-controlled transactions.
