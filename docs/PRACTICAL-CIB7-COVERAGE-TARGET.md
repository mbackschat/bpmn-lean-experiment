## Objective and boundary

Make ordinary business processes executable across the practical BPMN elements and depth implemented by CIB Seven `2.2.0`, with Lean and TypeScript agreeing on their meaning and Temporal preserving that meaning durably. [PROJECT-DESIGN](PROJECT-DESIGN.md#practical-cib7-coverage) owns the objective and [CIB7 ceiling](PROJECT-DESIGN.md#cib-seven-220-breadth-ordering). Historical MUE checkpoints supply reusable evidence; they do not establish this broader result.

The required unit is a behavioral variant in a useful combination: for example, a parallel Multi-Instance Sub-Process whose instances contain both human and automated work, rather than the presence of a Multi-Instance XML marker. Bounds and excluded combinations remain explicit. A row cannot close while its representative journey depends on a private capability, a probe-only business handler, or a hard-coded business-model identity.

## Optional integrations and exclusions

Optional integrations are Java delegate or REST drop-in compatibility, CIB external-task fetch-and-lock APIs, connectors, execution/task listener APIs, every scripting language, arbitrary beans/host-object access, vendor administration/history storage and production-scale qualification. Optional integration does not make a required Task family optional: Script and Business Rule task closure still needs an explicit usable execution contract, while its language/provider choice remains governed by the [compatibility scope](CIB-SEVEN-COMPATIBILITY-SCOPE-PROPOSAL.md).

The [source-grounded exclusions](research/CIB-SEVEN-BPMN-BREADTH-RESEARCH.md#cib7-depth-exclusions) remove Complex Gateway, Ad-Hoc Sub-Process, Standard Loop Characteristics and asynchronous compensation from this target. General standard DataObject/DataStore/DataAssociation execution is not inferred from CIB extension mappings. Automatic transport inferred from Collaboration Message Flows, full protocol-controlled transactions and universal formal proofs are not selected. Preserve and disclose existing project behavior outside the ceiling, including [standard MI output aggregation](CIB-BPMN-RELATION-REGISTER.md#cib-lim-0001-standard-sequential-multi-instance-output-aggregation-is-absent); it cannot count as CIB7 depth.

## Completion criteria

For each required variant, retain a linked semantic owner and requirement disposition, exact CIB source support and classified compatibility evidence when claimed, source admission plus independent Lean/TypeScript execution, applicable Temporal recovery/refinement/replay, and a project-owned whole business model. Public registration adds its capability/restriction row, exact pipeline binding, generated corpus map and Product 2 disclosure atomically under the [corpus contract](../model-corpus/README.md#admission-and-product-readiness-gate).

Cross-family acceptance must exercise data-bearing mixed human/automated branching and rework; repeated/nested Calls; both Multi-Instance Sub-Process modes; time/message-driven interruption and non-interrupting work; and exception/compensation recovery. A family cannot be marked complete solely because its isolated fixtures pass. Each selected combination has both an ordinary success path and the nearest wrong-order, wrong-owner, lost-data or stale-command discriminator.

Maintain separate BPMN requirement, CIB profile and whole-model evidence measures. Failed, deferred or unrun hosting and release gates stay open even when semantic gates pass. Final coverage acceptance requires all required variants and journeys to be evidence-closed with restrictions disclosed, followed by the owner decision defined in [PROJECT-DESIGN](PROJECT-DESIGN.md#practical-cib7-coverage); release versioning is separate.
